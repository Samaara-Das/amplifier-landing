- File name: `og-image.png`
- Dimensions: 1200 × 630 px
- Format: PNG (JPG acceptable)
- The file is referenced in `index.html` as `/og-image.png` (served from the site root)

## Suggested content

- Dark background matching the site (`#0f172a` slate-950)
- Amplifier logo/wordmark
- One-line headline: "Get paid to post. Get real people to post for you."
- Platform logos: LinkedIn, Facebook, Reddit
- Indigo accent color (`#4f46e5`)

## IMPORTANT

Do NOT share `tryamplifier.com` publicly (social posts, email, etc.) until this image is uploaded to the VPS at `/var/www/amplifier-landing/og-image.png`. Without it, LinkedIn and Twitter link previews will be broken (the meta tag points to `/og-image.png` which will 404).
