Everything you get

Set up

A brief in minutes

  • Paste your website. AI reads up to 10 pages and writes the brief in four parts.
  • No website? Answer a six-line form.
  • Add photos and files. Set the tone, phrases to include and things to avoid.
  • Edit every answer. Nothing is pre-ticked or chosen for you.

Find

Creators come to you

  • Creators browse Open campaigns and apply.
  • You see platforms, followers, niches, region, trust score, record and a note.
  • Choose Accept or Skip for each one.
  • Browse the creator directory with search and filters. Invite creators by hand.
  • Creators can also pitch you with a short note.

Post

A different post for each platform

  • AI writes each post from your brief.
  • AI makes the images: a lifestyle photo, or a real-life scene around your own product photo.
  • The creator reviews each draft, or switches on auto-post.
  • Posts go out from the creator's own accounts.
  • Creators use their own free AI keys. You pay no AI cost.

Track

Every sale ties to a creator

  • Each creator gets a code for each campaign.
  • LinkedIn and Facebook posts carry a tracked link. It goes only to your landing page.
  • Instagram and Reddit posts show the code. Buyers enter it at checkout.
  • Report sales by hand on the website, or by API. The same order ID never counts twice.
  • See sales by creator and by post.

Pay

You choose how to pay

  • Per sale, per performance, or both. A campaign runs at most 3 months.
  • The dashboard shows balance, impressions, engagement, spend, sales and cost per sale.
  • See numbers per creator and per platform.
  • Get a budget alert at 80%.

Safe

Checked before it goes live

  • Each campaign gets a quality score. It must reach 85 of 100.
  • An AI brand-safety check runs next. A person reviews it if the AI is unsure.
  • Edited text on a live campaign is checked again.
  • Every post carries the ad disclosure (#ad by default).
  • An admin approves each reported sale before anyone is paid.

Pricing

Set the pay. Add the budget. Pay for results.

Sign-up is free. There is no monthly fee. You pay only for approved sales and counted numbers, not for posts.

You say what counts as a sale: a purchase, sign-up, install, booking or waitlist join.

Pay per performance. Set a price per 1,000 views, per like, per comment and per repost. Add a cap per post if you want. Amplifire counts new numbers once a day. You can also use both pay types.

Money is set aside first. Put in a campaign budget of $50 or more before the campaign goes live. Creators are paid only up to the budget. Unspent budget returns to your balance when the campaign is cancelled or ends.

Refunds. Creator earnings wait 7 days after approval. A refund in those 7 days cancels the commission and returns the cost to your budget.

FAQ

Questions you should ask.

Is there a monthly fee? +

No. Sign-up is free. You pay only for approved sales and counted numbers, plus Amplifire's 20% on top. At a $10 commission, one sale costs $12.

Do I need a website? +

No. Without a website, you answer a six-line form. With one, the AI reads up to 10 pages and writes your brief. You can edit every answer.

Does Amplifire pick creators for me? +

No. Creators browse Open campaigns and apply. You see each application and choose Accept or Skip. You can also browse the creator directory and invite creators by hand.

Is Amplifire an affiliate network? +

No. Affiliates write their own posts. With Amplifire, AI writes a different draft for each platform. The creator reviews it and posts it from their own account.

Is the engagement fake? Are these bots? +

No. Every post comes from a real person, on their own account, through their own logged-in browser. We never sell, run or warm up accounts. Each post carries the ad disclosure (#ad by default).

How do you keep my brand safe? +

Each campaign gets a quality score. It must reach 85 of 100. An AI brand-safety check follows, and a person reviews it if the AI is unsure. A campaign never goes live unchecked. Each draft is also checked for length, banned phrases and being too similar to an earlier post before it is saved.

How do you stop fake numbers? +

Each creator has a trust score. Fake numbers lower it. Reported impressions are capped at the larger of 5,000 or 50 per follower. A post link must belong to the creator's connected account. A deleted post loses its pay.

How do you track sales? +

Each creator gets a code for each campaign. LinkedIn and Facebook posts carry a tracked link. Instagram and Reddit posts show the code, and buyers enter it at checkout. You report each sale with the creator's code and the order ID, by hand or by API. The same order ID never counts twice. An admin approves each sale before anyone is paid.

What if a buyer gets a refund? +

Creator earnings wait 7 days after approval. If you report a refund in those 7 days, we cancel the commission and return the cost to your campaign budget.

Which platforms do you cover? +

LinkedIn, Facebook, Reddit and Instagram (image posts). For now, Reddit posts go only to trading communities. Reddit suits finance and trading products.

Can I pay by card today? +

No. Amplifire is in a closed pilot. Card top-ups and bank payouts run in test mode until we go live.
