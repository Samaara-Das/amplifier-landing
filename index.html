Frequently asked questions

How much can I earn? +
Earnings depend on your engagement rate, which platforms you post on, and the campaigns you're matched to. Payouts are calculated per verified engagement (likes, comments, reposts). Creators in niches like finance, tech, and business tend to earn more because those audiences are more valuable to advertisers. There's no cap — the more engaging your posts, the more you earn.
What platforms are supported? +
Amplifier currently supports LinkedIn, Facebook, and Reddit. X (Twitter) support is coming soon once we've completed the native API integration. TikTok and Instagram are on the roadmap for after our AI video generation module ships. We expand platforms based on where our creators' audiences actually are.
How does payment work? +
Payouts are processed via Stripe Connect. Your earnings are held for 7 days after a post goes live (this protects against fraud and post deletions). Once released, you can withdraw anytime your balance is at least $10. Stripe deposits directly to your bank account. Companies are billed as creators earn, via their on-platform balance.
Is it free? +
Completely free for creators. Download the app, connect your accounts, and start earning — no subscription, no credit card required, ever. Companies pay per engagement, with a 20% platform fee applied on top of creator payouts. There are no setup fees, no monthly retainers, and no minimum spend.
