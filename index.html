FAQ

Questions you should ask.

What does it cost? +

Starting is free, with no card. You pay only when a sale happens: the commission you set, plus 20% for Amplifier. At a $10 commission, one sale costs $12. There is no monthly fee and no setup fee.

Is the engagement fake? Are these bots? +

No. Every post comes from a real person, on their own account, through their own logged-in browser. We never sell, run or warm up accounts. Each post carries the paid-partnership label (#ad) that the FTC requires.

Is my brand safe? +

Every campaign is screened before it goes live, and every post is checked before it is published. A campaign that fails the screen is held for review. It never goes live unchecked.

How do you track sales? +

Each creator's posts carry a tracked link. When someone does what you count as a sale, the sale is credited to the creator whose link brought them. You see every sale by creator and by post. Creators' earnings wait 7 days before they can withdraw, which gives you time to catch fraud.

Which platforms do you cover? +

LinkedIn, Facebook, Reddit and Instagram.
