How it works

Four steps. You stay in control.

  1. 01

    Install the app and connect your accounts

    Connect LinkedIn, Facebook, Reddit and Instagram. The app uses your own logged-in browser. Our server never sees your passwords.

  2. 02

    Accept campaigns

    The app shows campaigns that match your niche and platforms, with the commission for each sale. Take the ones you like.

  3. 03

    Review the posts, or let the app post

    AI writes a post and an image for each platform. Approve, edit or skip each draft. Or turn on auto-post.

  4. 04

    Earn for every sale

    You keep 100% of the commission. Earnings wait 7 days, then you withdraw them through Stripe.

FAQ

Questions creators ask.

Does it cost me anything? +

No. There is no fee for creators. You keep 100% of the commission a company sets for each sale. The company pays Amplifier's 20% on top.

Do you see my passwords? +

No. The app runs on your PC and posts through your own logged-in browser. Our server never sees your passwords.

Do I have to approve every post? +

No. You can review each draft before it posts, or let the app post on its own. You choose.

When do I get paid? +

Earnings wait 7 days. After that you can withdraw them through Stripe.

Do my posts say they are paid? +

Yes. Each post carries the paid-partnership label (#ad) that the FTC requires.
