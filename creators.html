Everything you get

Find work

Apply, or get invited

  • Browse Open campaigns. Use search and filters.
  • Apply with an optional note of up to 300 characters. Withdraw any time.
  • Companies can invite you. You can also pitch a company with a short note.
  • You can be on up to 3 campaigns at once, with 3 applications waiting.

Post

Drafts that fit each platform

  • The app writes a different post for each platform, with images.
  • It uses free AI keys that you paste in, such as Gemini, Mistral or Groq.
  • Approve, edit or skip each draft. Or switch on auto-post.
  • Posts go out in peak hours, with a random delay.
  • Every post carries the ad disclosure (#ad by default).

Get paid

You keep 100%

  • Pay is per sale, per performance, or both. You see it before you apply.
  • Performance pay is per 1,000 views, per like, per comment or per repost.
  • Earnings wait 7 days after approval. Then withdraw through Stripe from $10.
  • A refund in those 7 days cancels that earning. A deleted post loses its pay.

Private

Your passwords stay with you

  • The app runs on your own laptop.
  • It posts through your own logged-in browser.
  • Our server never sees your passwords.
  • The app is for Windows only.

Profile

Be easy to find

  • A profile meter shows what to add.
  • "See what companies see" previews your profile.
  • Companies can find you in the creator directory.

Fair

Rules that protect honest creators

  • Each creator has a trust score. Fake numbers lower it.
  • Reported impressions are capped at the larger of 5,000 or 50 per follower.
  • A post link must belong to your connected account.
  • Each draft is checked before it is saved.

FAQ

Questions creators ask.

Does it cost me anything? +

No. It is free for creators. You keep 100% of the pay the company sets. The company pays Amplifire's 20% on top.

Are there age limits? +

Yes. You must be 18 or older.

Is Amplifire an affiliate network? +

No. Affiliates write their own posts. With Amplifire, your app writes drafts. You review them, and they go out from your own accounts.

Do you see my passwords? +

No. The app runs on your laptop and posts through your own logged-in browser. Our server never sees your passwords.

Do I have to approve every post? +

No. You can review each draft before it posts, or switch on auto-post. You choose.

Does it work on a Mac? +

No. The app is for Windows laptops only.

Can I work with more than one company? +

Yes. You can be on up to 3 campaigns at once, with up to 3 applications waiting.

When do I get paid? +

An admin approves each sale first. Then your earning waits 7 days. After that, withdraw it through Stripe from $10. The company sets money aside first, and you are paid up to the campaign budget.

Amplifire is in a closed pilot. Card top-ups and bank payouts run in test mode until we go live.

What if the buyer gets a refund? +

A refund in those 7 days cancels that earning. The wait keeps pay fair for the companies that pay you.

Do my posts say they are paid? +

Yes. Every post carries the ad disclosure (#ad by default).

Which platforms can I post to? +

LinkedIn, Facebook, Reddit and Instagram (image posts). For now, Reddit posts go only to trading communities. Reddit suits finance and trading campaigns.
